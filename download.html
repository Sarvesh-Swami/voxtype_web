How to install

Speaker isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to confirm a couple of things the first time.

  1. Download the file. If your browser asks whether to keep it, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open it from the download notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs from your browser. When asked, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks about an unrecognised app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Open Speaker and follow the setup guide. It takes about two minutes.
Android 13 and later: the Speaker auto-type switch starts out restricted for apps installed from a website. The setup guide shows the one-time fix.

Updating

Download the newer version here and install it over the one you have. Don't uninstall first: your settings and keys are kept. After updating, open Speaker and tap Start floating bubble again.

What's new in 1.0.0

  • AI rewrite uses a draft card: your words collect next to the bubble, you can edit them, and the whole text is rewritten at once when you tap Send.
  • Long-press the bubble for a quick menu to switch AI rewrite on or off, or open the app.
  • When no text box is selected, the result is copied to the clipboard.
  • Recent dictations: optionally keep your last 50 dictations and their AI versions on the phone.
  • Model lists load from each provider and show only the models your key can use.
