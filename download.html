How to install

VoxType isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to confirm a couple of things the first time.

  1. Download the file. If your browser asks whether to keep it, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open it from the download notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs from your browser. When asked, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks about an unrecognised app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Open VoxType and follow the setup guide. It takes about two minutes.
Android 13 and later: the VoxType auto-type switch starts out restricted for apps installed from a website. The setup guide shows the one-time fix.

Updating

Download the newer version here and install it over the one you have. Don't uninstall first: your settings and keys are kept. After updating, open VoxType and tap Start floating bubble again.

What's new in 1.1.0

  • Speaker is now VoxType. Same app, new name. Install this version over the old one: your settings and keys are kept.
  • New Characters store: pick who floats on your screen. Vox, Shade, Noir, Kitsune or Volt, all free. Each one leans on the edge of your screen, pulls his headphones down and types on his laptop while you talk.
  • Save your own AI rewrite prompts with a name, and switch prompts from the app or from the bubble's quick menu.
  • AI rewrite uses a draft card: your words collect next to the bubble, you can edit them, and the whole text is rewritten at once when you tap Send.
  • Long-press the bubble for a quick menu to switch AI rewrite on or off, change the prompt, or open the app.
  • When no text box is selected, the result is copied to the clipboard.
  • Recent dictations: optionally keep your last 50 dictations and their AI versions on the phone.
  • Model lists load from each provider and show only the models your key can use.
