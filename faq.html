Installing

Why isn't VoxType on Google Play?

It's on its way. Until then you can install it from this website, and we'll link to the Play listing once it's live.

Android warns me about installing the file. Is that normal?

Yes. Android shows that warning for any app installed from outside Google Play. VoxType has no ads or trackers and only asks for the permissions it needs. You can check the file against the values on the download page.

How do I update?

Download the newer version from the download page and install it over the one you have. Don't uninstall first, so your settings and keys are kept.

Can I switch to the Google Play version later?

Yes. If Android won't install the Play version over this one, uninstall VoxType first, then install it from Play. You'll need to enter your keys and settings again.

Using VoxType

My words go to the clipboard instead of the text box.

Auto-typing is switched off. Open Settings → Accessibility → VoxType auto-type and switch it back on. If it keeps turning off, see the next question.

The bubble disappears, or auto-typing keeps switching off.

Your phone is closing VoxType in the background to save battery, and Android then switches off its Accessibility service too. Set battery use to Unrestricted, turn on Auto launch or Autostart if your phone has it, and lock VoxType in Recents. See Background & battery.

The auto-type switch is greyed out.

That's Android 13's protection for apps installed outside Google Play. Go to Settings → Apps → VoxType → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then try again. See Restricted settings.

Auto-typing doesn't work in one particular app.

A few apps, such as some terminals and games, don't use standard text boxes. In those apps VoxType copies your words to the clipboard. Long-press and choose Paste.

Does dictation stop when I pause?

No. VoxType keeps listening until you tap the bubble again, so you can stop to think.

Which languages are supported?

That depends on the engine. Most online engines handle dozens of languages and detect them automatically, or you can set a code such as en, hi or en-IN. The built-in engine supports the languages installed for your phone's speech recognition.

Costs & privacy

Is VoxType free?

Yes. No ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. The built-in engine costs nothing. If you use an online engine or AI rewrite, you use your own key and pay that provider directly for what you use. Some providers have free tiers.

Can you see what I say?

No. VoxType has no servers. Your voice goes either to your phone's own speech service or straight from your phone to the provider you chose. See the privacy policy.

Why does VoxType use Accessibility?

Accessibility is the Android feature that lets an app type into text boxes in other apps, like a keyboard does. VoxType uses it only to type your dictated words. It doesn't read or collect your screen content.

Where are my API keys stored?

Only in VoxType's private storage on your phone. They're left out of backups and device transfers, and each key is sent only to the provider it belongs to.
