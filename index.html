Features

Your voice, every app.

Simple enough to use in seconds. Flexible enough to fit the way you work.

Floating microphone

A bubble that sits over every app. Tap to start, tap to stop, drag it wherever you like.

AI rewrite

Say it your way, check the draft, then send it once for clean, punctuated text in your own style. Works with OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Groq or your own server.

How it works

Three steps. Zero typing.

01

Install Speaker

Download the app and follow the quick setup card: microphone, bubble and auto-typing.

02

Open any app

Messages, email, notes, a browser. Tap into the box where you want to write.

03

Tap & talk

Tap the bubble and speak. Your words appear as you pause. Tap again when you're done.

AI rewrite

Say it messy.
Send it clean.

Your words collect in a draft card next to the bubble. Fix anything you like, tap Send, and the whole thing is rewritten at once, with full context.
