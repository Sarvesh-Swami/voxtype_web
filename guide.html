Permissions

When you first open Speaker, the Finish setup card lists four steps. Tap each one in turn.

StepWhat to doWhy
MicrophoneTap Allow, then While using the app.To hear you while you dictate.
Display over appsTap Allow, find Speaker and switch it on.Shows the floating bubble.
Auto-typingTap Enable, read the note, tap Agree & continue, then switch on Speaker auto-type.Types your words into the text box.
Run in backgroundTap Allow and choose Unrestricted.Stops the phone closing Speaker.

Auto-typing is optional. Without it, Speaker copies what you said to the clipboard. In Accessibility settings, Speaker auto-type may be under Installed apps or Downloaded apps.

Restricted settings

Android 13 and later protect Accessibility for apps installed from outside Google Play. If the Speaker auto-type switch is greyed out:

  1. Try to switch it on once so Android shows the message, then close it.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Speaker.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu and choose Allow restricted settings. Confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
  4. Go back to Settings → Accessibility → Speaker auto-type and switch it on.

Background & battery

Some phones close apps in the background to save battery. When that happens, the bubble disappears and Android also switches auto-typing off.

Realme, Oppo, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Vivo and Samsung: set battery use to Unrestricted, turn on Auto launch or Autostart for Speaker if your phone has it, and lock Speaker in the recent-apps screen.

Speech engines

EngineKeyNotes
Android built-inNot neededFree. Your phone's own speech recognition.

OpenAIRequiredAccurate transcription models.
Google GeminiRequiredGemini models.
DeepgramRequiredNova models.

For an online engine, create an API key on the provider's website and paste it under API keys. Tap Refresh beside Model to load the models your key can use. Leave Language blank to detect it automatically. Providers charge for usage under their own terms; Speaker itself is free.

The floating bubble

Tap Start floating bubble. The bubble appears at the edge of the screen, with a Speaker notification while it runs.

ActionWhat it does
TapStart or stop dictation
DragMove the bubble
Long-pressQuick menu: AI rewrite on or off, or open Speaker

You can also use the Dictate button in the notification, or add the Dictate tile to Quick Settings.

AI rewrite

  1. In AI rewrite, switch it on, pick a provider (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq or custom) and a model, and add the key under API keys.
  2. Tap the bubble and speak. Your words collect in a draft card next to the bubble.
  3. Tap the text in the card to fix anything with the keyboard.
  4. Tap Send or the bubble. It shows Processing, then the rewritten text is typed in, or copied to the clipboard if no text box is selected.
Draft cardWhat it does
✕Discard the draft
MoreKeep talking and add to it
SendRewrite the whole draft. If it fails, the draft is kept and you can retry.

Recent dictations

With AI rewrite on, switch on Keep recent dictations on this phone to keep your last 50 dictations with their AI versions, ready to copy. They stay only on your phone, and switching the setting off clears them.
